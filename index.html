EEG2Face Neural Reconstruction

EEG2Face is a groundbreaking neural interface that transforms raw brain signals into dynamic 3D facial expressions in real-time. The system processes both raw and filtered EEG inputs through dual neural encoders, extracting emotion-aligned latent representations.

Using contrastive learning and the FLAME 3D morphable face model, EEG2Face learns to map neural activity directly to facial mesh vertices, enabling accurate reconstruction of emotional expressions without requiring any camera input from the user.

This patented technology opens new possibilities for patients with communication disabilities to express themselves through thought-controlled facial avatars.

Recognition Software

AMBT-Emotion is a multimodal emotion recognition software that jointly analyzes EEG signals, speech audio, and facial video to predict human emotional states in a robust and computationally efficient way.

The system is powered by the Adaptive Multimodal Bottleneck Transformer (AMBT), enabling stable cross-modal fusion with minimal additional parameters, making it suitable for both research experiments and real-world applications.

Zero-Calibration Emotion AI

Our proprietary multimodal framework eliminates the need for lengthy patient training sessions, delivering 95% comparable accuracy to traditional supervised methods instantly.
