1. Introduction to the Project  
Brain –Computer Interfaces (BCIs) enable direct communication between the human brain and external 
devices by decoding neural activity, allowing paralyzed patients to interact with their surrounding 
environment. Recently, BCIs have been designated as one of China’s six key future strategic 
technologies, highlighting their importance as a core next -generation technology.  
This project aims to develop reliable, scalable, and practical BCI software and hardware solutions , 
supporting next -generation neurotechnology and commercialization- ready applications.

5. Commercialization Progress  
Commercialization is currently underway through coordinated collaboration among  
Nazarbayev University, GL Co., Ltd., and the Daegu –Gyeongbuk Medical Innovation 
Foundation (DGMIF) . The consortium is actively participating in  Korean government -
funded R&D and commercialization programs , having continuously submitted business and 
commercialization proposals in  2024 and 2025 to secure public funding and accelerate market 
entry. In particular, the project was submitted to the  2024 First Integrated Call for the Health 
and Medical Technology R&D Program of the Korean government , with continued 
participation planned for  2026. 
Commercialization activities are conducted through  joint development, operational 
management, and continuo us performance validation  among the collaborating institutions.  
DGMIF serves as a primary application and validation site , where system performance is 
continuously monitored in real -world medical and rehabilitation environments. Commercial 
deployment is be ing pursued in parallel in Kazakhstan and Korea: in Kazakhstan through 
institutional adoption by university hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and public healthcare 
facilities, and in Korea through collaboration with medical institutions and healthcare par tners 
within established regulatory and clinical infrastructures. This dual -market strategy supports 
stable domestic deployment and scalable expansion across both regions.  
In addition, a  dedicated product website is currently under development by Nazarbay ev 
University  to support commercialization activities. The developed technologies are being 
modularized into independent functional components , which will be provided as  
customizable solution packages  to interested companies and educational institutions.
